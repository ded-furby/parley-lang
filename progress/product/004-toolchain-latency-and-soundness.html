Latency — measured, then cut

The 047 build-cost target

Of a 0.194 s parley check, 0.096 s was Lark rebuilding its LALR tables on every invocation, and a warm-unchanged run paid cargo ~0.3 s to conclude nothing changed. The tables now cache per grammar hash, and each program keeps its last binary keyed by sha256 of exactly what produced it.

The soundness campaign

Adversarial probing, wrong answers first

let fn be 1 beside let fn_p be 2 compiled both names to one Rust variable and printed 4 instead of 3 — no error anywhere. The escape scheme is now provably injective, and every other probe that reached rustc or produced nonsense became a named check-time diagnostic.
